How to book

Booking in 4 steps

  1. 01

    Check the menu

    Look through the menu and prices, then tap Book via LINE to open Kouhei’s official LINE account and add him as a friend.

  2. 02

    Choose a time on LINE

    Message Kouhei in English with the service you want and your preferred date and time. He will reply with available slots and send you an invoice for the ¥3,000 deposit.

  3. 03

    Pay the deposit

    Kouhei sends you a link to an online invoice (Square) on LINE. Pay the ¥3,000 deposit by credit card. Your booking is not confirmed until the payment is complete.

  4. 04

    Booking confirmed

    Once your payment is complete, Kouhei confirms your booking on LINE. Questions — like the color you have in mind — are welcome in English there anytime.

Please read before booking

Deposit & Cancellation Policy

  • Deposit: ¥3,000 (JPY), the same for every booking. It is a prepayment — the ¥3,000 is deducted from your total on the day.
  • Confirmed on payment. Your booking is confirmed only when the deposit payment is complete.
  • Non-refundable. The deposit is not refunded for any reason.
  • No changes. Once confirmed, your date and time cannot be changed.
  • Late arrival. If you are 15 minutes late, your booking is treated as cancelled and the deposit is not refunded.

You must agree to this policy to continue to booking.
